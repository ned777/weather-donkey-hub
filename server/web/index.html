The formulas

°F → °C
C = (F − 32) × 59

Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (× 0.556).

°C → °F
F = C × 95 + 32

Multiply by 9 and divide by 5 (× 1.8), then add 32.

Why those numbers? Water freezes at 32°F / 0°C and boils at 212°F / 100°C. That's 180 Fahrenheit degrees for every 100 Celsius degrees, so each °C is 180 ÷ 100 = 1.8 °F (the same as 9 ÷ 5), and the scales start 32 apart.

Do it in your head

  1. Quick °C → °F: double it, add 30.20°C → 40 + 30 = 70°F (really 68). Close enough for weather.
  2. Quick °F → °C: subtract 30, halve it.80°F → 50 ÷ 2 = 25°C (really 26.7).
  3. Exact °C → °F: double, take off 10%, add 32.25°C → 50 − 5 = 45, + 32 = 77°F ✓
  4. Exact °F → °C: subtract 32, halve, add 10%.77°F → 45 → 22.5, + 2.25 ≈ 24.8°C (really 25).
  5. The 10-degree ladder: every 10°C is 18°F.0°C 32°F10° 50°20° 68°30° 86°40° 104°Count from the nearest rung: 5°C = 9°F, 1°C ≈ 2°F.
  6. Flip-the-digits pairs:16°C ≈ 61°F and 28°C ≈ 82°F.
